Which headphones work?

Headphones with a built-in motion sensor that iOS can read. If the needle doesn't follow when you nod during setup, your pair has no sensor — the camera side check still works.

Does Nivela watch with the screen off?

Yes, while a desk sound is playing (rain, desk fan, deep hum or slow waves). iOS keeps apps that play sound running. With the sound off, Nivela watches only while it is open on screen.

Why didn't it nudge me when I looked down?

On purpose: a nudge plays only after you've leaned past your angle for the whole delay you set (30 seconds by default). Quick glances never count.
