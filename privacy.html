Privacy Policy

Nivela: Posture Level · Updated October 6, 2026

Nivela is a head-tilt level. It reads how far your head leans forward from the motion sensor in supported headphones, and it can measure your ear-to-shoulder line with the camera. This policy explains exactly what that involves.

What Nivela reads, and where it stays

  • Headphone motion. Nivela reads the tilt (pitch) of your headphones through Apple's Core Motion while you are watching. It turns those readings into three numbers per day — minutes upright, minutes at your working angle and minutes leaning — plus how many nudges played. These totals, your zero point and your settings are stored in a single file on your iPhone. They are never uploaded.
  • Camera (side check). When you start a side check, Nivela looks at the live camera frame for a few seconds and uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to find your ear and shoulder. Only the resulting angle is kept. The frame is not saved, not stored in your photo library and never leaves your iPhone.
  • Desk sounds. Sounds are generated on the device. Nivela does not use the microphone.

Nivela has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no third-party analytics. We do not sell or share personal data.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. To check which plan is active, Nivela uses Adapty (adapty.io), a subscription service that receives an anonymous app-generated identifier, your purchase receipts from Apple, the device model, iOS version and country derived from the App Store. Adapty processes this data on our behalf only to validate purchases and show the right plan. Motion data, side-check results and daily totals are never sent to Adapty.

Health information

Nivela is a habit tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it does not read or write Apple Health data.

Deleting your data

Deleting Nivela removes everything it stored. To remove subscription records held by Adapty, email us from the address below and include the ID shown in the feedback email Nivela prepares in Workbench › Send feedback.

Children

Nivela is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
