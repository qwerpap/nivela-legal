What Nivela is

Nivela shows the forward tilt of your head measured by supported headphones and can play a chime when you have leaned past your chosen angle for a while. It is a habit and awareness tool. It is not a medical device, gives no medical advice and is not a substitute for a professional. Readings depend on your headphones, how you wear them and your zero point, and may be inaccurate.
